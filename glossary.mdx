---
title: "Glossary"
description: "A shared list of terms and their approved translations, surfaced in the editor."
---

A glossary defines terms that should be translated consistently across a repository. Matching terms
are highlighted in the editor, so a translator sees the approved translation at the point of use
rather than having to look it up.
